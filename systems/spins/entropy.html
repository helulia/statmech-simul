Entropy during equilibration

An ensemble of $N$ independent chains of $\ell$ spins evolves in time: each step, every chain picks one random spin and flips it with probability $\tfrac12$ (the dynamics of numerical exercise 1). The Shannon entropy of the ensemble's magnetization distribution, $S(t) = -\sum_M \Pr(M)\log_2 \Pr(M)$, rises toward the steady-state value where all $2^\ell$ microstates are equally likely.
