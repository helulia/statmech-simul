Spins in a field: two ensembles

The same chain of $N$ independent spins $s_i=\pm\tfrac12$, now with an energy $E = -h\,M = -h\sum_i s_i$. Units: $k_B = 1$ and $h$ sets the energy scale, so the only knob is the dimensionless $\beta h$.

Canonical: fixed temperature, fluctuating energy

A heat bath at inverse temperature $\beta$ makes each microstate appear with weight $e^{-\beta E_\nu}$, so each spin is up with probability $p_\uparrow = \dfrac{1}{1+e^{-\beta h}}$ — this is the promised link $p \leftrightarrow \beta h$ from the CLT page.

Microcanonical: fixed energy, counted states

Isolate the chain at energy $E$. With the ground state all-up, $E-E_0 = h N_\downarrow$, the degeneracy is $W = \binom{N}{N_\downarrow}$, and $S = k_B \ln W$. The slope gives the temperature: $\beta h = \ln(N_\uparrow/N_\downarrow)$ — negative once $N_\downarrow > N/2$.
