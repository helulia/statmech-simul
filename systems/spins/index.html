A chain of non-interacting spins

A chain of $\ell$ independent spins $s_i = \pm\tfrac12$, each up with probability $p$; the total magnetization is $M = \sum_i s_i$. Here $h$ is an external magnetic field coupling to each spin with energy $-h\,s_i$: it biases the chain — $h > 0$ favors ↑ (pushes $p$ above $\tfrac12$), $h < 0$ favors ↓ — and at $h = 0$ the spins are unbiased, $p = \tfrac12$. Histograms are built from $N$ independent sample chains.
